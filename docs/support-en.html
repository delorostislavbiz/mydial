Common questions

MyDial does not open in incognito mode.
In incognito, click the MyDial button on the browser toolbar. First allow the extension in incognito in the extensions settings.

Will my bookmarks change?
Yes. Tiles are the bookmarks from your bookmarks bar. Before experimenting, save a backup with the button in MyDial settings.

Why access to sites?
Only for logos and screenshots. The permission is optional and the browser asks about it separately.
